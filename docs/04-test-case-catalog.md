## How to read an entry

- **Title.** The test plan defines no separate title for a test case: the **scenario** sentence is its identifying description and is quoted verbatim.
- **Preconditions and steps** are not defined separately in the test plan. They restate what the scenario, the plan’s test-data rules (§1.4) and, where named, the canonical flow (§3) say. Where they and the scenario differ, the scenario wins.
- **Expected result** is the part of the scenario after “→”, verbatim; scenarios without an arrow are themselves the expected outcome. All error responses are asserted on HTTP status and `error.code` only (plan §1.3).
- **Status** is one of: _Automated and traceably linked_ (a committed Playwright test carries the `@TC-…` tag and the linkage is confirmed in both directions); _Automation status requiring investigation_ (linked, but the test does not exercise the scenario as written); _Manual by design_ (the plan explicitly marks it _Automation: No_, with a reason); _Not yet automated_ (the plan intends automation; no test exists yet).
- **Recommendation** lines are recommendations of this audit, not established facts or approved plans.
- **Demo users:** Alex = `emp-alex` (EMPLOYEE), Sam = `emp-sam` (EMPLOYEE), Jordan = `rev-jordan` (REVIEWER).
